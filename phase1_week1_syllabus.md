# Phase 1, Week 1: Linux & Terminal Fundamentals (Travel Curriculum)

This guide is designed for watching and learning on an iPad while traveling, without needing a laptop.

---

## 1. Ready-Made YouTube Playlists (One-Click Access)

You can open these directly in the YouTube app on your iPad and tap **Save Playlist** (the `+` or bookmark icon):

### Primary Playlist: NetworkChuck – Linux for Hackers (and everyone)
* **Link:** [NetworkChuck Linux Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLIhvC56v63IKrRHh3gvZZMANvCsOhwrAW)
* **Why this is best for iPad/Travel:** NetworkChuck is high-energy, visual, and breaks Linux down into bite-sized 10–15 minute episodes. It teaches the command line without feeling like a dry university lecture.
* **Episodes to watch for Week 1:**
  1. *EP 1: Linux for Hackers // (FREE Linux course for beginners)*
  2. *EP 2: Navigation & Directories (`pwd`, `ls`, `cd`)*
  3. *EP 3: Creating and deleting files/folders (`touch`, `mkdir`, `rm`)*
  4. *EP 4: Users and Permissions (`chmod`, `chown`)*

## 4. Hands-on on iPad (No Laptop Needed!)

If you get 10 minutes of downtime on your iPad and want to type commands directly without installing anything:
* Open Safari and go to **[Killercoda](https://killercoda.com)**
* You get an instant Linux terminal inside the browser that works smoothly with an iPad touch keyboard.
